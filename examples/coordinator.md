# Example: a community garden report

## New coordinator

Use $project-orchestration to coordinate a short report comparing two community garden layouts. Work in this project's canonical workspace. First recover `brief.md` and `HANDOFF.md` if present. Reuse existing workers where useful. Have one worker compare layouts using the supplied measurements; after accepting that analysis, brief a writer with its actual findings. The final deliverable is `outputs/recommendation.md`, with assumptions and the reasons for the recommendation. Keep internal prompts in English and communicate with me in my language.

## Existing coordinator

Read $project-orchestration and adopt the relevant coordination guidance. Preserve accepted results and the current task scope. Update the existing handoff at the next meaningful milestone; do not restart research or create additional workers merely to adopt the skill.
